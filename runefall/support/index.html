Playing

How do I play?

Swipe a standing stone to topple it. It lands across as many tiles as it is tall and knocks over every stone it lands on. Light every rune before you run out of pushes.

Can I see where a stone will land first?

Yes. Tap a stone to see its reach, then tap a highlighted tile to push it that way. Hold a finger on open ground to see through the stones, and pinch to zoom on crowded islands.

How do I earn three stars?

One for finishing, one for finishing within the gold pips at the top of the screen, and one for lighting the gold rune before the last ordinary rune.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint once to see which stone to start with, and again for its direction. Undo and Restart are always free. Settings → How to play explains every kind of stone.

The full game

What's free?

Chapter I (10 islands) is free, including its Trials. A single one-time purchase unlocks the other 19 chapters, the Daily Stone and all later Trials. There are no ads, timers or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. You can turn music, sound effects and vibration on or off in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Your purchase is kept by Apple and comes back with Restore purchase.
