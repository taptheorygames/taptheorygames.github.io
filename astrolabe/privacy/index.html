Privacy policy: Astrolabe: Clockwork Puzzle

TapTheory Games · Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Astrolabe app uses and why. Short version: we don’t ask for your name or email, there are no accounts, and your progress stays on your phone.

Information stored on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, mastery crowns, best moves, daily streak, hints left), your settings and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved on your device only. TapTheory Games does not receive them. A second copy is kept in the app’s own storage on the same phone, so progress survives if the system clears web data. Uninstalling the app deletes both.

Adverts (Google AdMob)

The free version shows adverts provided by Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra hint, and occasional adverts between levels. To show and measure adverts, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device and app information and how you interact with adverts. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later. On iPhone, the app asks once, just before the first advert, whether apps may track you; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising ID is not shared and adverts are not personalised. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google’s policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (App Store)

If you buy “Remove Ads”, payment is handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether your Apple ID owns the purchase.

Analytics

This version of the app does not send analytics to us.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can turn off personalised adverts in your phone’s settings, decline consent where asked, and delete all data by uninstalling. Questions or requests: taptheorygames@gmail.com.
