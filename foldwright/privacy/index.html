Privacy Policy

Foldwright: Jewel Garden (iPhone) · Effective 23 November 2026

The short version: Foldwright does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and needs no internet connection to play. Your progress is saved only on your own iPhone.

Who we are

Foldwright: Jewel Garden is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers.

The game makes no network requests of its own and contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media. The only online step is Apple's own in-app purchase service, described below.

What is stored on your iPhone

So you can carry on where you left off, the game saves on your device: the puzzles you have solved, stars, best turns, medals, achievements, Daily Jewel results and streak, settings (sound, music, vibration, motion, contrast, text size) and whether you own the Full Garden. It can also keep an optional playtest summary (which puzzles took longest) that never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself. This data stays on your iPhone, is never sent to us or anyone else, does not identify you and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

Sharing and backups

If you tap Share today's result, Export progress or Playtest report, the game opens the iPhone share sheet. You choose where, if anywhere, the result or file goes; nothing is sent to us.

In-app purchase

The first three gardens and the Daily Jewel are free. The optional one-time "Full Garden" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The game only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can open the other gardens, including when you tap Restore purchase.

Apple and the App Store

The game is downloaded through Apple's App Store or TestFlight. Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases, crashes and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install and crash counts. TestFlight testers can also choose to send feedback and screenshots to us through TestFlight.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • To erase your progress, open Menu and choose Reset progress, or delete the app.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. You are still welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the game handles information (for example adding adverts or online features), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
