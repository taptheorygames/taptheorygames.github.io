Privacy Policy

Tapnetic: Magnet Puzzle (iPhone) · Effective 24 November 2026

The short version: Tapnetic does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and no internet connection of its own. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Tapnetic: Magnet Puzzle is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, email taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. It makes no network requests of its own and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves your progress (levels, stars, best results, daily streak), your settings and whether you have unlocked the full game. A second copy is kept in the app’s own storage on the same iPhone so progress survives if iOS clears web data. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, does not identify you, and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple’s own policies.

In-app purchase

The one-time “Full Game” unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, the game sends your daily-puzzle result, your total stars and the achievements you earn to Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings; we do not receive or store it. You can turn Game Center off in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications

The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. If you do, iOS asks for your permission and your iPhone schedules a local notification at 9:00 each day. It is created on your device; no server is involved and nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a daily result, the iPhone share sheet opens with a short text (date, moves and stars). It only goes where you choose to send it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
