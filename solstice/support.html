Playing

How do I play?

Umbra can only stand in shadow. Tap a shaded tile, or swipe, to step. Turn the sun a quarter turn with the two sun buttons, or by swiping in a circle around the board. The sun only turns if Umbra will still be in shade afterwards. Reach the Moon Gate to clear the courtyard.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint. Each free courtyard has three hints; the full game has unlimited hints. Undo is always free, and Restart resets the courtyard.

The whole temple

What's free?

The first 15 courtyards are free. One one-time purchase, Unlock Full Game, opens all 60 courtyards and unlimited hints. No adverts or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new device

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. Sound, music and vibration can be switched in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account, and deleting the app deletes your progress.
