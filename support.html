Help with The Lighthouse Keeper

Need help or found a bug? Email taptheorygames@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a puzzle

Tap Hint to see one piece to turn. Hints cost one lamp oil. You earn oil by finishing new puzzles, keeping the Night Watch, and from some achievements. You can also watch a short advert for +2 oil.

A gate has shut and I can't finish

In Chapter 5, lighting lamps in the wrong order can shut a gate for good. Tap Undo or Reset to try another order. Hints are free while you're shut out.

How does the tide work?

In Chapter 4, every move moves the tide. Mirrors with wave marks go under water, and light passes straight over them. Tap Wait to let the tide move without turning anything.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone. It stays when you close the game, but it is deleted if you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset all progress.

Can I change my advert choices?

Yes: Settings → Privacy & adverts. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Daily reminders

Turn them on or off in Settings → Daily reminder, or in your phone's notification settings.
