Privacy Policy

Moonlock: Tide Puzzle (iPhone) · Effective 23 November 2026

The short version: Moonlock does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and no internet connection of its own. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Moonlock: Tide Puzzle is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, email taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. It requests no iPhone permissions, makes no network requests of its own, and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves your puzzle progress, best results and settings using standard on-device app storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, does not identify you, and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

Settings → Progress backup lets you copy your progress as text. That text only goes where you choose to paste it.

In-app purchase

Chapter 1 is free. The optional one-time "Full Atlas" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The game only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made so it can unlock the other chapters, including when you tap Restore purchase.

Apple and the App Store

Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install or crash counts. These cannot identify you.

Children

Moonlock is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all saved progress. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
