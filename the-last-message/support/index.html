Playing

How do I play?

Read the messages as they arrive and choose how Alex responds. Tap Examine on a photo and log only what it really shows. On evidence boards, tap a claim, then the record that tests it, then choose what the pair actually proves. Tap How to play on the title screen for the full briefing.

I'm stuck on a board or a deduction

Every board has a free hint. Hints never cost money; they only lower your case grade a little. Wrong answers are explained, and you can always try again.

How long is a case?

Case 001 takes about 15 to 25 minutes. There are four endings; the best one has to be earned with careful, first-try reasoning and Detective Hassan's trust.

What does my grade mean?

Your grade (out of 100) is itemised on the result screen: deductions, evidence boards, witness challenges, supported observations and how carefully you preserved evidence.

Sound, text and screen

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't set to silent (Ring/Silent switch or Action button), turn the volume up, and check that the soundtrack is switched on in Settings. Sound starts after your first tap.

The text is too small, or the animations bother me

Open Settings on the title screen and switch on Larger text or Reduced motion.

Can I play in landscape?

No, the game is designed for portrait (upright) play on iPhone.

Progress and purchases

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Continue investigation picks up where you left off.

How do I start again?

On the title screen, tap Start a new case and confirm. Endings and achievements you've already unlocked are kept.

Does progress move to a new phone?

Progress is stored on the device. It may come across if you restore your new iPhone from a backup of the old one.

I bought a case. How do I get it on another iPhone?

When later cases are available, use Restore purchases in the game while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Content

Is this game suitable for my teenager?

It is rated 13+. The story involves a faked death, identity fraud, coercion and a medical cover-up. Death is referred to but never shown, and there is no profanity or gore.
