Playing

How do I play?

Swipe up, down, left or right. While your finger is down an arrow shows the direction; slide back to cancel, lift to dash. Settings → Instant Dash makes swipes fire immediately.

A room feels impossible

Every room is solvable. Some need the in-maze HUD: the [||] or [SKIP] button can drop into the maze as a block.

Purchases

What's free?

Rooms 1–200 are free. The optional one-time "Full 1,000-Room Licence" opens rooms 201–1,000 and all skins. "Executive Override" is an optional consumable purchase that skips the current room once. There are no ads or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open the store (star button, top right) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. You can turn sound, vibration and narrator notes on or off in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
