Privacy Policy

Solstice: Shadow Puzzle does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

TapTheory Games · Effective 23 November 2026

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings (sound, music, vibration) and whether you have unlocked the full game are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment details or address. The game only asks Apple whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock the game and restore it on another device. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Internet

The game plays fully offline. It connects to the internet only when you buy or restore the purchase, and then only to Apple's App Store.

Children

Solstice is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
