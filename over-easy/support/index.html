Playing

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen to aim (a dotted line shows the arc), then let go to fling the egg. Tap while it is in the air to tuck and drop straight down.

I fell and lost my progress

Falls of 5 metres or more send the egg back to its last checkpoint. That is the game. Cartons act as checkpoints; Hard-Boiled mode removes them.

Purchases

What's free?

The first 25 metres are free. The optional one-time "Full 1,000m Tower" purchase opens the rest, Stage Warp and Hard-Boiled mode. The "Prestige Egg Shell Pack" is a separate, purely cosmetic purchase. There are no ads or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open the shop (egg button, top right) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. You can turn sound, vibration and narrator notes on or off in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
