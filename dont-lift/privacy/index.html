Privacy Policy

Don't Lift: 1,000 Trials (iPhone) · Effective 27 November 2026

The short version: Don't Lift does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and no internet connection of its own. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Don't Lift is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, email taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. It requests no iPhone permissions, makes no network requests of its own, and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves your progress, best results and settings using standard on-device app storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, does not identify you, and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

Share cards and short fail clips are only created when you tap Share or Clip, and only go where you choose to send them.

In-app purchases

Trials 1–150 and the Daily Gauntlet are free. The optional one-time "Full Lab Clearance" opens trials 151–1,000 and the cursor skins. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The game only asks Apple whether a purchase has been made so it can unlock content, including when you tap Restore Purchases.

Apple and the App Store

Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install or crash counts. These cannot identify you.

Children

Don't Lift is rated 9+. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all saved progress. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
