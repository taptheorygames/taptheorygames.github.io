Playing

How do I play?

Swipe on the board, or tap North, East, South or West. Sun slides with the tide, Moon slides the opposite way and Star slides a quarter turn clockwise. Every vessel keeps sliding until it hits stone, the shore or another vessel. Bring each one to its matching dock.

Can I see where they'll go first?

Yes. Hold a tide button, or start a swipe and pause, to see every vessel's path. Slide back to the middle to cancel. Plan mode does the same with a separate Send button.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint once for a clue and again for the exact tide. Undo and Restart are always free.

The full atlas

What's free?

Chapter 1 (10 puzzles) is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks the other four chapters, the 8 Tide Trials and the daily tide. There are no ads, timers or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open Settings (top right) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. You can turn sound effects, music and vibration on or off in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. To keep a copy, use Settings → Progress backup.
