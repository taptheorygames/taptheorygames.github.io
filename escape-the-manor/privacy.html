Privacy policy: Escape the Manor

TapTheory Games · Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Escape the Manor app uses and why. Short version: Escape the Manor does not collect any personal data. There are no adverts, no tracking and no accounts, and your progress stays on your phone.

Information stored on your phone

Your game progress (rooms, stars, coins, hints, achievements, Daily Lock streak), your settings and your reminder time are saved on your device only. TapTheory Games does not receive them. A second copy is kept in the app’s own storage on the same phone, so progress survives if the system clears web data. Uninstalling the app deletes both.

No adverts, no tracking

The game contains no adverts and no advertising or tracking software. It does not use your advertising ID and never asks to track you across other apps or websites. We do not share any information with third parties.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

If you buy the “Full Manor” unlock, payment is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether your account owns the unlock.

Daily reminders

If you switch reminders on, the app schedules them on your phone (at most one a day). No information leaves your device for this.

Analytics

This version of the app does not send analytics to us. If a future version adds Google Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage events such as “chapter completed”), this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game is aimed at players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can delete all data by uninstalling the app. Questions or requests: taptheorygames@gmail.com.
