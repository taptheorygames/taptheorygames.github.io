Playing

How do I play?

Tap North, East, South or West, or swipe anywhere on the screen. Gold flies one perch with that wind straight away. Blue flies the wind you chose on the previous turn (on your first wind, Blue waits). Land Gold on the diamond and Blue on the moon after the same wind.

How do I know where Blue will go?

A faint ghost of Blue sits on the perch it will reach next, and a small moon marks the wind key Blue will repeat. "How to play" in Settings replays the introduction.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint once for a clue and again for the exact wind. Undo is always free.

The whole garden

What's free?

The Glasshouse chapter (10 gardens and their Moonflights) is free. One one-time purchase opens Moonwater, the Bell Conservatory, the Star Causeway, the Dawn Orchard, the archive gardens and every Moonflight. No adverts or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new device

Open Settings (top right) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. Chimes, music and vibration can be switched in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
