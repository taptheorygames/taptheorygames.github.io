Playing

How do I play?

Tap ↶ or ↷ (or swipe across the board) to turn the whole garden a quarter turn. Gravity then pulls the golden medallion down the screen until something stops it. Guide it into its turquoise seal. Menu › How to play explains every special tile.

Can I see a move before I make it?

Yes. Hold a turn button for half a second to preview where everything lands. Release on the button to make the move, or slide your finger off to cancel. Dashed outlines on the board show what bridges, the crane, clockwork walls and magnets will do next.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint: first it shows what to watch, then it lights up the next turn, then it offers a step-by-step guide. Undo and Restart are always free.

How do stars work?

Three stars for solving a garden in par turns or fewer, two within two turns of par, one for any solution. An insight medal is for par without hints.

The Full Garden

What's free?

The first three gardens (30 puzzles) and the Daily Jewel are free. A single one-time purchase opens the other seven gardens and the Encore. There are no ads, timers, lives or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open Menu › Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. The ♫ button mutes everything; music, effects and vibration each have their own switch in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Moving to a new phone

Menu › Settings › Export progress, send the file to yourself, then Import progress on the new phone. Your purchase comes back with Restore purchase.
