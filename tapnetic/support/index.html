Tapnetic support

Something not working? Email taptheorygames@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I play?

Tap a magnet to pulse it. Red magnets pull every loose piece in their row and column; blue magnets push them away. Pieces slide until something stops them. Fill every socket with an orb to win (gold orbs go in gold sockets). Settings (top right) has a guide to every piece.

I’m stuck

Use Undo, Restart or Hint. A hint shows the next move of the shortest solution; that run can then earn two stars at most. If a position can no longer be solved, the game offers to rewind to the last solvable one.

How do stars work?

Three stars for solving in par moves or fewer without a hint, two stars for up to two moves over par, one star otherwise. Par is the true shortest solution, checked by the built-in solver.

Restoring my purchase

Go to Settings › Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. The purchase screen has a Restore button too.

Daily reminder

Turn it on in Settings › Daily reminder. If you previously said no to notifications, allow them for Tapnetic in the iPhone Settings app.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements need you to be signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings › Game Center). The game works fully without it.
