Runefall

Topple stones. Wake the runes.

A stone falls exactly as far as it is tall, and everything it lands on falls too. Swipe a standing stone to topple it across a floating island and light the runes beneath. Line up one push so that it sets off a chain across the whole island.

200 islands in 20 chapters add turnstones, heavy basalt, twin stones, sunstones, rollstones, skystones that leap, backstones that fall back at whatever hits them, and deep runes that must be struck twice. There is a new Daily Stone every day, Trials for each chapter and a logbook of your feats.
