Cradle & Cog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · TapTheory Games

Cradle & Cog is a single-player puzzle game that works entirely offline. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting service, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (unlocked stages, stars, best times) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, accessibility options) are saved on your iPhone only, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it in the game under Settings › Reset all progress.

Purchases

Cradle & Cog is a paid app with no in-app purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
