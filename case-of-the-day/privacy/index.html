Privacy Policy

Case of the Day (iPhone) · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version: Case of the Day does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and needs no internet connection. Your progress is saved only on your own iPhone.

Who we are

Case of the Day is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers.

The game makes no network requests and contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your iPhone

So you can keep your streak and pick up where you left off, the game saves on your device: the day you started playing, the cases you have played and solved, stars, XP, rank, badges, trophies, your streak, weekly goals and settings (sound, music, vibration, text size, reminder time). This data stays on your iPhone, is never sent to us or anyone else, does not identify you and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

Daily reminders

The game can remind you once a day when a new case opens. It asks for permission to send notifications first, and only if you agree. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us. You can change the time or switch them off in the game's Settings, or in your iPhone's Settings under Notifications.

Feedback and sharing

If you tap Send feedback, the game opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent unless you send that email, and we only see what is in it. If you tap Share on a result, the game opens the iPhone share sheet with your result; you choose where, if anywhere, it goes.

Purchases

This version is free and has no in-app purchases. If we add any later, payment will be handled entirely by Apple, and we will update this page first.

Apple and the App Store

The game is downloaded through Apple's App Store or TestFlight. Apple may collect information about downloads, crashes and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install and crash counts. TestFlight testers can also choose to send feedback and screenshots to us through TestFlight.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • To erase your progress, open Case Files and choose Reset all progress, or delete the app.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. You are still welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the game handles information (for example adding adverts, purchases or game statistics), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
