Moonlock: Tide Puzzle

Three vessels. One tide. Never the same direction.

Every tide moves all of your vessels at once. Sun sails with the tide, Moon sails against it, and Star turns a quarter clockwise. Swipe a direction, watch them slide until stone, shore or another vessel stops them, and bring each one home to its dock.

50 hand-built puzzles across five chapters of a flooded observatory add sluice gates, anchor beds, paired tunnels and pressure-plate doors, plus 8 expert Tide Trials and a daily tide.
