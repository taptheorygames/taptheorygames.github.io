Playing

How do I play?

Hold one finger on START and drag. The orb follows slightly above your finger so your thumb never hides it; change the offset in Settings. Do not lift until the orb reaches RELEASE.

My phone vibrated and I lost

Some trials buzz on purpose as a harmless distraction. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Purchases

What's free?

Trials 1–150 and the Daily Gauntlet are free. The optional one-time "Full Lab Clearance" opens trials 151–1,000 and the cursor skins. There are no ads or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open the store (diamond button, top right) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. You can turn sound, vibration and narrator notes on or off in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
