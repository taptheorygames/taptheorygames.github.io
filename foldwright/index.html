Foldwright: Jewel Garden

A calm puzzle about one simple rule: turn the whole garden a quarter turn, and gravity pulls the golden medallion down. Guide it into its turquoise seal.

Ten jewel gardens, each with its own twist: folding bridges, mirror portals, one-way shutters, memory gates, twin medallions, fragile paper, copper magnets, a clockwork heart and a living paper crane. Every puzzle is checked by a solver, with a par to beat and hints that teach before they tell. A new Daily Jewel every day, and an original koto-and-flute score for each chapter.

The first three gardens and the Daily Jewel are free. One optional purchase opens every garden and the Encore. No adverts, no timers, no lives, no account. Plays offline, and your progress stays on your phone.
