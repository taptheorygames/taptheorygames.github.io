TAPNETIC: Magnet Puzzle

Tap a magnet. Everything in line moves.

Red magnets pull every loose piece in their row and column. Blue magnets shove them away until something stops them. Orbs roll straight over sockets unless you plan where they’ll land, so every tap is a little bit of physics and a lot of thinking ahead.

Then the magnets start moving each other: live magnets fire by themselves when they’re moved, and one tap can set off a whole chain reaction. 24 levels in four chapters, a new daily puzzle every day, and endless Free play.
