Privacy Policy

The Little Pink Dragon (iPhone) · Effective 16 November 2026

The short version: The Little Pink Dragon does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases and no internet connection. Your child's game progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

The Little Pink Dragon is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your child's privacy, email us at taptheorygames@gmail.com.

Who the game is for

The Little Pink Dragon is designed for young children, roughly three years old and up, playing with a parent or other trusted adult. We built it so that a child can play without any information about them ever leaving the device.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect any personal information, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. The only place to type anything is a grown-ups’ confirmation box used to reset saved progress; what is typed there is checked on the device and is never stored or sent. The game does not request any iPhone permissions.

The game makes no network requests. It works completely offline, and it contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your device

To let your child carry on where they left off, the game saves a small amount of game-progress data on your iPhone using standard on-device app storage. This includes which world and scene was reached, which activities are complete, and simple play-time counters used by the game. This data:

  • stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else;
  • does not identify your child;
  • is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

The game also uses your device's speaker for sound effects and, where supported, gentle vibration. Neither of these involves collecting any data.

Apple and the App Store

The game is downloaded through Apple's App Store or TestFlight. Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as the number of installs or crashes). These statistics cannot identify you or your child, and we use them only to improve the game.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, we do not need, and do not ask for, parental consent to collect data. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. The game contains no advertising, no links out of the app, no purchases and no chat or social features.

Your choices

  • To erase your child's saved progress, open Settings & Help in the game and choose Grown-ups: Reset saved progress, or delete the app from your iPhone.
  • Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. We will never start collecting personal information from children without first obtaining verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.
