Privacy Policy

The Last Message: Case Files (iPhone) · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version: The Last Message: Case Files does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking and no internet connection. Your progress is saved only on your own iPhone.

Who we are

The Last Message: Case Files is made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: taptheorygames@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. The game does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. It requests no iPhone permissions. The "messages" in the game are part of the story; the game cannot read or send your real messages.

The game makes no network requests and contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media.

What is stored on your iPhone

So you can continue where you left off, the game saves on your device: your case progress and choices, evidence found, grade, endings and achievements unlocked, settings (sound, text size, reduced motion), and simple play-time counters. This data stays on your iPhone, is never sent to us or anyone else, does not identify you and is deleted when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example to iCloud), Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's own policies.

Test versions (TestFlight)

Test versions of the game can show three optional questions at the end of a case and a "Copy playtest report" button. The report contains only game statistics (ending, grade, time spent per scene, your answers and any note you type). It is copied to your clipboard only if you tap the button, and reaches us only if you choose to paste and send it. These features are not part of the public App Store version.

Purchases

Case 001 is free. Later cases may be offered as in-app purchases. If you buy one, payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The game only asks Apple whether your account owns an unlock, so purchases can be restored on your devices.

Apple and the App Store

The game is downloaded through Apple's App Store or TestFlight. Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases, crashes and app performance under Apple's Privacy Policy, which we do not control. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install and crash counts. TestFlight testers can also choose to send feedback and screenshots to us through TestFlight.

Children

The game is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • To erase your progress, choose Start a new case on the title screen, or delete the app.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete. You are still welcome to contact us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
