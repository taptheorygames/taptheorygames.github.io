Astrolabe: Clockwork Puzzle: Support

TapTheory Games

How do I play?

SWAP pins the other end of the astrolabe. CW and CCW swing the gem 90° around the pin. Land the gem on every star, then on the crown. Levels 1-1 to 1-3 guide you step by step; Settings › Replay Guided Tour shows the guide again.

I’m stuck on a level

Every level has been checked by a solver and can be finished in its par number of moves. Tap Hint for the next move (3 free hints a day, or watch a short video for another). Undo is always free.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings (the gear) › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your phone. It is lost if you uninstall the app. Remove Ads can always be restored from Settings.
