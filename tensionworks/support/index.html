Playing

How do I play?

Drag from a brass orb to a pin to attach a wire (or tap the orb, then tap the pin). Press Release and watch. When every green chime has rung, the level is solved. If it doesn't work, press Retry: your wires stay put so you can change just one.

What do the wires do?

Whip snaps back hard once and lets go. Tether never snaps and keeps pulling. Coil (from Chapter 4) is the strongest and the only wire that can launch iron orbs. Pick the wire type with the buttons above Release.

How do stars work?

Solve a level with par wires or fewer for three stars, one more for two stars, any more for one. Your best result is kept.

I'm stuck

Tap ? to show the next wire of a solution, including which wire type to use. Tap a placed wire to remove it, or use Undo and Clear.

The full workshop

What's free?

Chapter 1 (20 levels) is free. A single one-time purchase unlocks Chapters 2 to 5 (80 more levels). There are no ads, timers or subscriptions.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Restore is also on the unlock screen.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound, motion and progress

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and turn the volume up. Sound starts after your first tap. Sound, volume, haptics and reduced motion are in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Settings → Reset progress clears your stars (it asks you to tap twice).

Accessibility

Every pin is a button for VoiceOver and keyboards: select one pin, then a second to connect them. The game follows your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.
