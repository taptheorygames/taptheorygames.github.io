Case of the Day

A new mystery every day. Search the scene, question the suspects, catch the lie and make your one accusation before the case closes at midnight.

A short, cosy detective game for iPhone, set in the rainy harbour towns of Port Vesper and Lantern Quay. Each case takes about 10 minutes and has its own hand-painted scene and suspects. Sgt. Moss is there with hints when you are stuck.

Free. No adverts, no purchases, no account. Plays offline, and your progress stays on your phone.
