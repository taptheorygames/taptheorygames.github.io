Cradle & Cog – Support

Thanks for playing! If something is wrong or a stage feels unfair, please tell us at taptheorygames@gmail.com. Include the stage number and, if you can, your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play

  • Drag from the glowing crystal core, a weight or a brass stud to another one to attach a cable. Tapping one and then the other works too.
  • Pick the cable type with the buttons at the bottom of the screen. REEL winds the cable in, SPRING stretches and flings, and ROPE keeps its length for swings. New types unlock as you progress.
  • HOLD locks every reel at its current length, which is perfect for setting the size of a swing. Tap it again to resume.
  • RELEASE cuts every cable. Tap a single cable to cut just that one.
  • Deliver the core into the lit cradle. A padlock means you must do something first (break glass, press a plate, charge a ring or catch a spark).

Stuck?

Tap ? for a hint. Or open the pause menu (☰) and choose Watch a demo to see the solution play out with the real physics, then Stop demo · Play it to try it yourself.

Progress

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. It is kept when you update the app and removed if you delete the app.
