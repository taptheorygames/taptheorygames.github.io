Support

Stuck on a case or something not working? Here are answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, email us.

Contact: taptheorygames@gmail.com, or tap Send feedback in the game's Settings.
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the case you were playing. We usually reply within a few days.

Playing

How do I play?

Tap the glowing spots in the scene to find clues. Talk to each suspect; one of them is lying. Catch the lie by matching what they said to the clue that proves it. Then make your one accusation: pick who did it and the clue that proves it. New here? Play the two-minute training case with Sgt. Moss from the home screen.

When does a new case open?

A new case opens every day at midnight, in your phone's local time. Your first day in the game is case 1.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint and Sgt. Moss will point you in the right direction. Hints are free; using one means that case can't earn its third star.

How do stars work?

One star for the right person, one for the right clue and one for a clean investigation (no more than one wrong guess and no hints).

Can I replay old cases?

Yes. Open Case Files to play or replay cases. Your daily case and streak are not affected.

Streaks and reminders

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

Not always. A streak freeze saves it automatically; you start with one and earn more as you play. If you have none left, the game offers one chance to save your streak the next day.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open Settings in the game and tap Daily reminder. You can also switch notifications off in your iPhone's Settings.

Sound, text and screen

There's no sound

Check that your iPhone isn't set to silent, turn the volume up, and check sound effects and music in the game's Settings. Sound starts after your first tap.

The text is too small

Open Settings and choose a larger text size.

Progress

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your iPhone.

Does progress move to a new phone?

Progress is stored on the device. It may come across if you restore your new iPhone from a backup of the old one.

How do I start again?

Open Case Files and choose Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

Content

Is this game suitable for children?

The cases are gentle, cosy mysteries: missing items, ruined cakes, small thefts and harmless hoaxes. There is no gore and no profanity.
