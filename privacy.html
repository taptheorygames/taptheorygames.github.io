TapTheory Games

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 (previous version: 9 November 2026)

The short version. We don't ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress stays on your phone. Adverts only appear when you choose to watch one for a reward; they come from Google AdMob, which may collect some device information to show and measure them. Anonymous game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) help us improve the game. The optional purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

This policy explains what information is handled when you play mobile games made by TapTheory Games ("we", "us"), an independent game developer. It covers The Lighthouse Keeper (called Lighthouse Keeper: Mystery on the Apple App Store) on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and any other TapTheory game that links to this page, unless that game has its own notice.

The Little Pink Dragon has its own privacy policy. It shows no adverts and collects no data.

1. Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following only on your own phone (or in your web browser if you play a test version online). It is not sent to us.

  • Your progress: puzzles solved, stars, letters found, lamp oil, island items, achievements and looks, and whether you have bought the full story.
  • Your settings: music, sound, vibration and reminder time.
  • Your Night Watch streak and the days you played, used to keep streaks and to plan reminders.

You can delete all of it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the game. (A purchase is not lost: use Restore purchase to get it back.)

2. Adverts (Google AdMob)

The phone apps are free to download. On iPhone the only adverts are optional reward adverts from Google AdMob that you choose to watch (for example, for extra lamp oil or to keep a Night Watch streak); there are no pop-up adverts. Nothing advert-related starts until you first tap a reward advert. If you buy the full story, you get those rewards without watching adverts. When an advert is shown, to show, measure and limit adverts and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with adverts (views and taps).

Your choices:

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalised adverts are shown. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & adverts.
  • iPhone and iPad: the first time you tap a reward advert, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your IDFA and adverts are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Game statistics

Our phone apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous game events, such as a puzzle being started or finished, a hint being used, the shop or the unlock offer being opened, or a purchase being completed or restored, together with basic device information and an app instance ID. On iPhone we use the version of Firebase that does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and these statistics are not used for advertising or tracking. We use these totals to see which puzzles are too hard and to fix problems. They are not linked to your name or contact details, and we do not sell them. The test versions played in a web browser do not send any statistics.

4. Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your phone by your phone's own notification system. No server sends it and no notification token is collected. You can switch reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

5. Purchases

Chapters 1–3 and the Night Watch are free. On the Apple App Store, one optional in-app purchase ("The full story") opens Chapters 4–10 and the Lantern Trials and gives advert rewards without adverts. The payment is handled entirely by Apple (or Google Play, if offered there): we never see your name, card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was made, keeps that on your phone, and can ask the store again when you tap Restore purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. Looks in the shop are bought only with lamp oil earned in the game.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or store your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • There are no accounts, chat or social features in the game.

7. Children

Our games are general-audience puzzle games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

8. Keeping and deleting information

Information on your device is kept until you reset progress or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google for adverts and statistics is kept according to Google's own retention policies. You can ask us questions about your data, or ask us to request deletion of statistics linked to your app instance, using the contact below.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the UK, California or the United Arab Emirates), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold personal information on our own servers, most choices are made in the game's Settings and in your phone's settings, as explained above. For anything else, contact us.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.
