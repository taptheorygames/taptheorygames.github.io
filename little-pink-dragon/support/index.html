Playing together

How do we play?

Tap Let's Play! and follow the little hint at the top of each scene. Children pop bubbles by tapping, sweep a finger across several bubbles, hold a finger still on a moon pearl, drag treasures and rainbow bubbles, and trace simple paths. Each of the 20 worlds has 7 short activities that slowly add new kinds of play.

Can my child lose?

No. There are no timers to beat, no lives and no game over. Every scene finishes with a celebration.

What age is it for?

It's made for children around three and up, playing with a grown-up nearby. Early worlds need only a single tap, and later worlds add gentle new gestures.

Sound and screen

There's no sound

Check that the Ring/Silent switch (or Action button setting) on the side of your iPhone is not set to silent, and turn the volume up. Sound starts after the first tap on the screen.

The screen asks me to turn the phone upright

The game is designed for portrait (upright) play. Hold the iPhone upright, and if it doesn't rotate back, check that Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center isn't holding it sideways.

Progress

Is progress saved?

Yes. Progress saves automatically on your iPhone as your child plays, and Continue Adventure picks up where they left off.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Delete the app and install it again. This removes all saved progress on that device.

Does progress move to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on the device. It may move across if you restore a full iPhone backup, but this isn't guaranteed.

Privacy and safety

The Little Pink Dragon has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no links out of the app and no internet connection. It collects no personal information. Read the full Privacy Policy.
