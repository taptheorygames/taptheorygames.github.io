Escape the Manor: support

Thank you for playing. If something is not working, email taptheorygames@gmail.com with your phone model and the chapter you were in. We reply within a few days.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Tap the 💡 button in the top corner. Each chapter has three free hints, and if you stand still for a while, a sparkle shows you where to look next. Once you have unlocked the whole manor, hints are unlimited.

I bought the full manor on another phone

Open Settings › Restore purchase on the title screen (or the unlock sheet), signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I open Eleanor’s Letter Room?

Find all three hidden coins in every one of the 23 chapters. Replaying a chapter is a good way to pick up the ones you missed.
